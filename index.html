What I Solve

I build infrastructure that developers do not have to worry about.

My focus is the space between application code and reliable production delivery: cloud infrastructure, CI/CD automation, Kubernetes, Infrastructure as Code, observability, security, and operational efficiency.

I approach DevOps as an engineering discipline—not a list of tools. The goal is to make delivery safer, infrastructure reproducible, systems easier to operate, and cloud environments easier to scale and optimize.
